## Findings with numbers

1. **Event listener scheduling** (`input-event-listener/finding.json`): source events occur at frames 13 and 28 in both; Rive's listener response is also at frames 13 and 28, Anima's at 14 and 29. Delta +1 frame, twice. Anima explicitly specifies next-step listeners; this is a semantic difference, not a broken observation channel. The source key times are 5/24 and 11/24 seconds, away from host frame boundaries.
2. **Timeline boundary clocks** (`animation-key-event-target/boundary-probe/finding.json`): keys at 0.25, 0.5 and 1 seconds produce Anima frames 16, 31, 60 versus Rive 15, 30, 61. Deltas +1, +1, −1 frame. Float-clock/boundary handling differs. The completed probe instead tests independently authored identical off-grid times 5/24, 11/24, 23/24, at frames 13, 28, 58 in both. It does not claim boundary equivalence.
3. **Transition boundary clocks** (`events-transition-end/boundary-probe/finding.json`): at 0.5-second exit / 0.25-second mix, Anima exit/start at frame 31 and completion at 46; Rive 30 and 45. Delta +1 frame for all three reports. The completed state/transition probes use a 0.51-second exit / 0.25-second mix: both report exit then begin at 31 and done at 46. Boundary cases remain failures with independent native captures, not adjusted references.

No Rive source, .riv content or CLI documentation text was copied.
