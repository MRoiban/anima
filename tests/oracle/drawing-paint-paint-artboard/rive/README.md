oracle's 240 × 240 artboard (12 px per grid unit: board 240, component tiles 120, bar 24 wide,
keyed x 12 → 84 over 1 s). Captured locally with `~/.rive/bin/rive . --commands=commands.txt`
(CLI 1.9.1); frames at 0, 0.5 and 1 s.
