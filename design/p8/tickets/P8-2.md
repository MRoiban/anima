## Ticket P8-2 — inverse kinematics

Owns: new module `modules/ik/**` (runtime entry `ik`, `heavy: true`, budget 896 B), its wiring, the
`## ik` chapter in `spec/SPEC.md`, conformance `spec/conformance/rigging-{ik,ik-bend}/`, oracle probes
`tests/oracle/rigging-{ik,ik-bend}/`, a `NOTICE` paragraph, inventory status for these rows.
Read-only: `modules/rig/**` (use its `api.ts`; seams only, marked).

Rows: rigging.ik rigging.ik-bend.

Depends on: P8-1 merged into `p8-rigging` (branch from it).

Implement the `ik` constraint of DESIGN §3 through `registerConstraint` (in `init`):
- `target` (node, its world pivot), `bones` = chain length including the constrained bone (D12;
  1 = aim), `bend` `cw` (default) | `ccw` (D13), `strength` animatable/bindable. `writes()` returns
  the chain's bones so their descendants solve after the chain.
- 1 bone: rotate the bone so its direction points at the target. 2 bones: analytic solve by the law
  of cosines on the bones' world lengths (scaled), joint on the `bend` side (screen-clockwise side of
  start→target for `cw`); a negative-determinant ancestor mirrors the side; out of reach the chain
  straightens toward the target; coincident target and start leaves the rest pose.
- 3+ bones: follow Rive's MIT runtime's approach (`rive-runtime`, `src/constraints/ik_constraint.cpp`,
  read for the algorithm only, never copy text) with a **fixed** iteration count — no convergence
  early exit — so every runtime gets identical numbers (D14). Say so in the spec; add the attribution
  paragraph to `NOTICE` like the existing natural-rounding entry.
- Strength blends each chain bone's world transform with `blend` (rig's D15 rule).
- Diagnostics: `rig-ik-host` (on a non-bone), `rig-ik-chain` (`bones` longer than the chain);
  `rig-target` from rig for unknown targets. One test each.
- Example pair `modules/ik/examples/reach.animl` + `.ani`: the DESIGN §3 arm with a pointer-driven
  target (an input listener writing the target's `x`/`y`).

Acceptance:
- `npm run test:scope -- ik rig conformance oracle --browser=chromium` green; Firefox once.
- `rigging-ik`: 1-, 2- and 3-bone chains reaching reachable targets (tip within 1e-6 of target,
  lengths kept) and straightening for unreachable ones, at ≥ 3 target positions each, plus strength
  0.5 and an animated strength. `rigging-ik-bend`: `cw` and `ccw` elbow sides for targets right,
  below and left of the start, and a mirrored parent (`scaleX="-1"`).
- Oracle probes paired with Rive IK scenes (art converted into bone space): report raw numbers.
- `anima schema ik` lists the fields; `node tools/size.ts` exit 0 with `ik` ≤ 896 B, core+web unchanged.
